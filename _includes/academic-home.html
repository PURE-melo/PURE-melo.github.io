Guanchun Wang (王冠淳)

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Xidian University, and a master's supervisor. I received my Ph.D. degree in 2024 and B.Eng. degree in 2019 from Xidian University, both from the School of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Xiangrong Zhang. I am a member of the Key Laboratory of Intelligent Perception and Image Understanding of the Ministry of Education, led by Prof. Licheng Jiao.

My research interests include weakly supervised, multimodal and incremental learning, remote sensing image interpretation, and electromagnetic target intelligent interpretation. I have published more than **40 papers**, including over **10 first-author/corresponding-author papers** in CAS Q1 journals and CCF A conferences such as ICLR, ICCV, ACM MM, IJCAI, IEEE TIP, TNNLS, TCSVT, TGRS, GRSM, ISPRS and Pattern Recognition, with 1 ESI hot paper and 3 ESI highly cited papers, over **900 citations** in total (h-index 13, Google Scholar). I have also applied for or been granted more than 10 national invention patents.

I serve as a reviewer for ICLR, CVPR, ECCV, AAAI, IJCAI, ACM MM, IEEE TNNLS, TMM, TCSVT, TGRS, JSTARS, ISPRS and Pattern Recognition. My honors include the Xidian University Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award, the Best Poster Award of the AI and Remote Sensing Science Interdisciplinary Forum (AIRS-2025), and the Silver Award of the CETC Electromagnetic Big Data Challenge.

Research Interests

Zero-shot & Weakly Supervised Learning

Zero-shot and weakly supervised learning for object detection, semantic segmentation and scene classification.

Multimodal Large Models

Multimodal large models and parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and their application to remote sensing image interpretation.

Remote Sensing Image Interpretation

Object detection, land-cover segmentation and scene classification towards open-world and open-vocabulary settings.

Change & Anomaly Detection

Remote sensing image change detection and anomaly detection under zero-shot or weakly supervised conditions.

Electromagnetic Target Interpretation

Intelligent interpretation of electromagnetic targets in dynamic open scenarios: incremental learning, domain adaptation and few-shot learning.

Incremental & Domain Adaptation

Incremental, domain adaptation and multi-modal frameworks for remote sensing and signal modulation recognition.
